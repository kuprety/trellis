FAQ

Questions parents ask us Questions & answers

Is it free?

Yes! It’s free for returning parents. Mentors volunteer their time.

How long is my gap allowed to be?

Any length is welcome. Our mentors’ gaps range from six months to twelve years.

Can dads and other caregivers join?

Absolutely! Any parent or caregiver returning to work is welcome.

How are matches made?

We look at your field, the length of your gap, and your family setup, then pair you with a mentor whose path is closest to yours.

How much time does it take?

Most pairs talk for about an hour every two weeks. You and your mentor set the pace together.

Can I become a mentor later?

Yes. Many of our mentors started as mentees. Once you’re back at work and ready to give back, you can sign up to mentor.
